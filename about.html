我们做什么What We Do

巴特雅仕是一家位于广州的西服制造工厂，主要为国内批发市场档口、外贸公司与海外买家提供男士西装的生产与贴牌代工。合作方式分三条线：批发供货、OEM/ODM 贴牌、团体与个人定制。 BARTHOYASHI is a suit manufacturing factory in Guangzhou producing men's tailoring for domestic wholesale stalls, trading companies and overseas buyers. We work in three modes: wholesale supply, OEM/ODM private label, and group or individual bespoke.

工艺与品控Workmanship & Quality Control

决定一件西服卖相与返修率的，是这几处细节These are the details that decide how a suit looks on the rail and how often it comes back.

版型与放码Blocks & Grading

按目标市场体型调整版型，码段放码后再投产，避免大货走形。Blocks adjusted to the target market's body shape, graded before bulk cutting.

裁剪与对格Cutting & Matching

条纹、格料按对格要求裁剪，前片、领、袋盖纹路对齐。Stripes and checks cut to match across fronts, lapels and pocket flaps.

缝制与整烫Sewing & Pressing

工序分组流水，关键部位加固定针；成衣定型整烫后再检验。Section work with reinforced stitching at stress points; garments pressed and shaped before inspection.

中期与尾期验货Mid-line & Final QC

量产中期抽检，尾期按 AQL 抽验尺寸、外观与辅料，问题批次返修。Mid-production checks, then AQL-based final inspection of measurements, appearance and trims.
