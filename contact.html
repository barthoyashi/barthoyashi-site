发询盘时请带上这些What to Include in Your Enquiry

信息越全，报价越准，来回越少The more you send, the more accurate the quote and the fewer rounds it takes.

款式与数量Style & Quantity

  1. 款式图 / 参考样衣照片（正背面、细节各一张）Sketches or reference photos (front, back, details)
  2. 每款每色数量，是否需要分码Quantity per style and colour; size breakdown
  3. 目标价位区间与销售渠道Target price range and sales channel

面料与工艺Fabric & Workmanship

  1. 面料成分与克重要求（或"由工厂推荐"）Fibre content and weight, or "factory's recommendation"
  2. 粘合衬 / 半麻衬，是否需要全麻衬Fused / half-canvas / full-canvas
  3. 特殊工艺：手巾袋、真开扣眼、撞色里布等Details: ticket pocket, working buttonholes, contrast lining

品牌与包装Branding & Packing

  1. 主唛、洗标、吊牌文件（PDF / AI 均可）Main label, care label, hangtag files (PDF / AI)
  2. 折叠装袋 / 挂装 / 独立包装Flat-packed / hanging / individual packing
  3. 是否需要条码贴、外箱唛头Barcode stickers and carton marks

交期与交付Lead Time & Delivery

  1. 期望交货日期，是否需要分批Required date; split shipments or not
  2. 交货方式：工厂自提 / 国内物流 / FOB 口岸Ex-works / domestic logistics / FOB port
  3. 是否需要验货或第三方检测Inspection or third-party testing required?
